Good to know

A few courtesy details on billing and support, so nothing catches you by surprise later.

Taxes

Prices exclude tax — GST (+18%) in India, sales tax/VAT elsewhere. Shown at checkout and remitted by you; we're not the merchant of record.

SMS & WhatsApp

Prepaid on every plan, so you always know the cost in advance — no free credits, no surprise bills.

Payment rate (India)

UPI payments via our PhonePe partnership get more affordable as you scale. Outside India, you're welcome to connect your own Stripe or PayPal.

Enterprise SLA

Response times and uptime commitments are agreed in your contract, tailored to your needs rather than a one-size-fits-all number.
